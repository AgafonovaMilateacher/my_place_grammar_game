My Place Grammar Game

Explore the rooms, answer the questions and reach the finish!

1

Play in two teams. Blue and Red take turns rolling the dice and moving along the numbered path.

2

Complete a grammar task on every numbered space: choose a / an / some / any, correct a mistake or make a sentence.

3

Explore the rooms! On spaces 1, 3, 5, 9 and 15, answer three special questions aloud using there is / there are and prepositions of place.

4

Collect points: 1 for each correct grammar task or 3 for a completed room challenge.

5

Reach FINISH to win! The first team to finish wins. The team starting first changes with each new game.
